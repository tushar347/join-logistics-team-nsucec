What the team does

We give every CEC event its look

From the first sketch to the last piece of tape on the wall, the Art & Craft Team makes the club feel handmade.

Event décor

Stage backdrops, entrance arches and table pieces for club fests and competitions.

Posters and artwork

Hand-drawn and digital posters, banners and social art for every campaign.

Handmade gifts

Cards, crests and small crafts for guests, speakers and winners.

Craft workshops

Sessions where members learn a new technique and make something together.

Possible tasks

Show us with your hands

Pick the tasks that suit you best. Photos or scans of finished work are enough, and you choose them inside the application.

Drawing

Poster for an imaginary event

Design a poster for a CEC event that does not exist yet. Pencil, ink, paint or tablet, your choice.

Craft

Waste into art

Turn cardboard, bottle caps, old magazines or any scrap into something worth displaying.

Illustration

A mascot for NSU CEC

Draw a character that could stand for a computer and engineering club. Any medium works.

Paper craft

Cut-paper card or 3D piece

Make a themed greeting card, pop-up or small paper sculpture with clean folds and cuts.

Layout

Backdrop plan

Sketch a stage backdrop for a tech fest with rough measurements and a list of materials.

Live round

Quick sketch on the spot

A short timed sketch during the meet-the-team round. We give the theme, you bring the pencil.

Questions people ask

Before you apply

Who can apply?

Current members of North South University Computer and Engineering Club. We check your NSU ID against the member list when you apply.

Do I need art experience?

No formal training is needed. We care about effort, ideas and how you finish a piece.

What materials do I need?

Whatever you already have. Pencils, paper and scrap are enough. Digital work is welcome too.

How much time does membership take?

A few hours a week, with busier stretches before big club events. [CONFIRM WITH TEAM]

Can I apply twice?

Each member ID can apply once per round. To change an answer, ask a CEC executive.
